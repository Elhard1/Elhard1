- **AI CCTV.** Smart camera systems that detect people and vehicles and alert you. Runs on site, not in the cloud.
- **Online stores.** Custom built or WooCommerce shops with trade pricing, made to measure products and stock sync.
- **Support.** Ongoing maintenance and help from someone local.
